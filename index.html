COMMON QUESTIONS

What to know before we start.

Do you sell the vehicle directly?

No. MHR Auto Group acts as your independent buying and leasing concierge. The final purchase or lease is completed directly with the licensed selling dealer.

When is the $499 fee due?

As soon as we agree on numbers on a particular vehicle.

Can you guarantee the lowest price?

No ethical broker should guarantee a specific lowest price. We provide market-informed sourcing, negotiation, and transparent comparison to help you make a stronger decision.

Do you help with trade-ins or financing?

Yes. Our goal is to simplify the purchasing process for you.

What areas do you serve?

The initial focus is Dallas–Fort Worth and surrounding Texas markets, with remote deal-review support available more broadly where permitted.
